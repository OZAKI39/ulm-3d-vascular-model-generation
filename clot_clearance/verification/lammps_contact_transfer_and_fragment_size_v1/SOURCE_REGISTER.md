# Source register and interpretation

- [Chueh, Puri & Gounis, J NeuroIntervent Surg 9 (2017), 183-187](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26919972/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1136/neurintsurg-2015-012256): PubMed indexed abstract checked on 2026-10-08. Reports clot fragments >200 um in thrombectomy comparisons. Our >=200 um equivalent-volume statistic is a reporting reference, not a universal clinical danger threshold. Publisher/direct open access was restricted; no unavailable full-text detail was inferred.
- [Microtripsy in-vitro clot study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4528908/): indexed primary full text, Table 3/4 and Debris section checked. Most measured particles were below 10 um; the largest reported was 153 um in that experiment. This discrete 50 um spacing model cannot predict that fine debris distribution. The study's statements about embolic safety are not imported into the model.
- [Liu et al., JMPS 193 (2024), 105858](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmps.2024.105858): original project source register, paper/SI extracts, frozen material card and prior reviews reused. DOI resolver unavailable in this check; no new material fitting or claim of revalidation. Interface remains porcine-skin peeling proxy. K/G are the existing intact small-strain mapping; density/drag/contact radius are engineering assumptions.
- [LAMMPS pair bpm/peri](https://docs.lammps.org/pair_bpm_peri.html) and [bond bpm/peri](https://docs.lammps.org/bond_bpm_peri.html), release 30Sep2026 docs checked: companion pair force acts on nonbonded neighbors; bonds break irreversibly at the threshold. The optional onset factor is the existing local patch, not an official new material parameter.
- Pinned [pair source](https://github.com/lammps/lammps/blob/8de817dd79bfe4525d5d39246a212d833e6dee07/src/BPM/pair_bpm_peri.cpp), [bond source](https://github.com/lammps/lammps/blob/8de817dd79bfe4525d5d39246a212d833e6dee07/src/BPM/bond_bpm_peri.cpp): local source/executable/patch identity verified against previous build record. No LAMMPS C++ source changed this round.

All bibliographic thresholds are separated from simulation validity. The task does not establish patient-specific embolic risk, real bubble loading, or biological quantitative validation.
