6. conservative boundary-node volume quadrature
============================================================

对于一维 N nodes：

    w = h/2

interior:
    w = h

    V_ijk = wx_i * wy_j * wz_k

    h^3

    h^3 / 2

    L^3

    0.015625 mm^3
