============================================================
18. mechanical condensation for cone witnesses
============================================================

对于给定 phase trial p：

求最优 mechanical response u：

    Hxx u = -Hxs p

不要显式逆矩阵。

LEVEL 0 可用：

    scipy.linalg.solve

fine levels：

优先：

    scipy.sparse.linalg.cg

如果 Hxx SPD 已验证；

否则：

    minres

或合适成熟 solver。

然后形成：

    v = [u,p]

计算：

    Rayleigh = v^T H v / (v^T v)

任何：

    resolved Rayleigh < -curvature_tol

即可证明存在可行负曲率方向。

没有找到负 witness：

只能标：

    INSTABILITY_NOT_DETECTED

不能自动标：

    STABLE

除非 phase 仍全部 strong lower
且 Hxx 已正定。

============================================================
19. stability classification rules
============================================================

STABLE：

    first-order gates pass

AND

    all phase variables strong lower
    or interior directions fully resolved

AND

    minimum permitted curvature > tolerance

UNSTABLE：

    first-order gates pass

AND

    at least one verified feasible
    negative-curvature direction exists

UNRESOLVED：

    weak-bound cone exists
    but neither stability certification
    nor negative witness is reliable

不要把：

    no negative witness found

写成：

    stable。
