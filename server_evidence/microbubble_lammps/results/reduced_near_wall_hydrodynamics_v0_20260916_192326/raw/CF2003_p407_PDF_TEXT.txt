Figure 7 also shows the validity of the approximations for large gaps. Again, both the expansion
for large l/a and the Pad´e approximant, which are valid in principle for large gaps, also hold for

for large gaps.
Analogous results were obtained for the rotational velocity, again with a precision better than

2 per cent on the range ε ⩽0·1.
