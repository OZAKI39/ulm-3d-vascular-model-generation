The 2D reference is a design reference, not proof that the 3D algorithm is correct. Its nearest search includes primary-element choices and finite candidate acceleration; the 3D implementation must instead use deterministic geometry-derived ordering for reduction while never selecting an arbitrary triangle normal as the physical answer.

Planned qualification: triangulated planes including face/edge/vertex and alternate tessellations; cylinder/sphere mesh trajectories with physical-time refinement; sharp wedges of 30/60/90/120/150 degrees; rank-3 corner; deliberate nonmanifold failure. Reorder triangle storage and compare geometrically identified clusters and velocities. Independent Python reconstructs adjacency, normals and KKT projection. Preserve and strengthen complete fatal-trial diagnostics before every stop.

Parameter policy, frozen before trajectory tuning: tie tolerance uses the inherited coordinate-scaled 64*eps scale, factors 0.5/1/2. Smooth dihedral thresholds 10/15/25 degrees are numerical mesh parameters (production mesh p95≈12.34°, p99≈15.35°). These remain below the 30-degree change of the least sharp required wedge. Test all three; any material classification/trajectory sensitivity is reported PARTIAL, never tuned away. Local one-rings are bounded by topological connectivity and smoothness; no averaging across nonmanifold edges or disconnected opposing faces.

The previous tiny bounded position cleanup remains capped at margin/4, and requires a prior wall retry. For true multi-surface cases it is disabled: refine physical time. Swept geometry, not a projected normal inequality alone, decides acceptance.


## Finite-radius offset normal correction after the first LOW trial

The direct use of area-weighted mesh face pseudonormals at a convex shared edge was rejected by the real LOW regression: at t≈0.2363 s the mesh pseudonormal and the exact distance gradient differ by 1.32755 degrees. The projected velocity still decreases the true gap at 2.29208e-6 m/s. Bounded position cleanup must not compensate such a first-order error. The diagnostic and first implementation are preserved in validation/LOW_DEVELOPMENT_REGRESSION_DIAGNOSIS.json and provenance/development_v1.

We therefore distinguish the **mesh surface pseudonormal** from the **finite-radius contact normal**. Face interior uses the fluid-side face normal. At a single smooth edge/vertex patch, the former still uses area/angle-weighted incident face normals and establishes orientation; the latter is normalize(X minus the weighted candidate closest feature point), the gradient of the distance offset surface. No triangle is selected arbitrarily. The offset gradient is continuous through a convex feature Voronoi region and matches the face normal at its boundary. Multiple distinct patches keep their own face pseudonormals and independent inequalities. Diagnostics store both vectors. This is a deliberate refinement of the recommended pseudonormal recipe, required by the task's exact swept-sphere safety and tiny-correction-only condition; it adds no wall physics and changes no mesh vertices.
