Orthogonal sections of the B2 acoustic pressure amplitude at 2.5 MHz. The XY, YZ and XZ planes are extracted at Z=0, X=0 and Y=0 mm, respectively, from the existing complex acoustic field. Every section uses native 0.1 mm grid nodes without field smoothing or interpolation; the raster display uses nearest-neighbor sampling. The shared linear color scale is 0-39 kPa and represents |P|, the magnitude of the complex pressure phasor, not RMS pressure. The original magma purple-to-yellow color map is restored. White curves show exact intersections of the existing vessel mesh with each plane. The three panels preserve equal spatial aspect and approximately identical physical scale. Display ranges are X=[-3.2,10.2], Y=[-6.2,6.2] and Z=[-1.6,1.6] mm; X and Y limits match the preceding right-hand plane. The homogeneous-water acoustic model does not resolve vessel-wall material interfaces. No simulation was run and no original source data were modified. The figure contains no volume rendering, isosurfaces or volume ROI locator. PDF/SVG use raster field maps and editable vector labels and vessel outlines.
