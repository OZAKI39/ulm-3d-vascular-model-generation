Figure 1. Data-grounded PZT-to-microbubble workflow illustrated with the sealed Stage 4I B2 case. (a) Actual positions, normals and 6 mm diameters of four ideal disc sources, with source-pressure amplitudes, phases and enabled states. Blue and orange distinguish 0 and 180 degree source phases. The phasor uses p(t) = Re[P exp(i omega t)]; s_i is the source switch. This is an acoustic boundary-source representation, not an electromechanical PZT model. (b) Native k-Wave complex-basis superposition at 2.5 MHz in homogeneous water. Orthogonal slices in the indicated local region and the z = 0 mm amplitude section use the same linear 0-39 kPa scale. Vessel outlines provide geometric context and are not acoustic material interfaces. No spatial smoothing or intensity rescaling is applied. (c) Saved primary-force y-z components at t = 0: all 256 bubble centers are shown and arrows use every fourth global particle index (64 arrows) to reduce overlap. Arrow scale is 100 pN. The secondary diagram shows actual radii and separation of particle IDs 12 and 249, the closest initial pair with d/(R_i+R_j) >= 5. A rigid pair-axis coordinate transformation preserves geometry. Pair-force magnitude (54.29 pN) is reconstructed from the same response table, retaining all 8 volume harmonics and their h-squared derivative weights; arrow directions encode the computed attraction/repulsion and their graphical lengths are schematic. This selected separation does not establish complete far-field or experimental validity. (d) All 256 recorded trajectories over 0-6 s, with color denoting time and a cropped vessel view of the tracking region. Lines join saved states (20 ms spacing) and terminate at each recorded first-crossing event; they are not recovered integration substeps. The nominal integration timestep is 0.5 ms with adaptive displacement constraints. The equation describes unconstrained Stokes migration; RK2 with wall/contact constraints yields the stored motion. Counts distinguish branch arrival from observation-censored, unassigned particles. B2 ends with A = 76, B = 0 and unassigned = 180. Microbubble position markers are enlarged for visibility; trajectory coordinates are not magnified.

Model scope. The source runs are Stage 4I, not the earlier Stage-1 software demo. Existing limits on near-contact forces, near-wall hydrodynamics, quasi-steady drag and finite slip Reynolds number remain. Contact enforces geometric nonpenetration only and does not model adhesion or fusion. No figure establishes local physical accuracy. No new acoustic, CFD or trajectory simulation was run for these figures.
