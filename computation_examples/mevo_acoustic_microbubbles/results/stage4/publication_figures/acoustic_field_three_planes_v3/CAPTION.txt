Orthogonal sections of the B2 acoustic pressure amplitude at 2.5 MHz. The XY, YZ and XZ planes are extracted at Z=0, X=0 and Y=0 mm, respectively, from the existing complex acoustic field. Every section uses native 0.1 mm grid nodes without field smoothing or interpolation; the raster display uses nearest-neighbor sampling. The shared linear color scale is 0-39 kPa and represents |P|, the magnitude of the complex pressure phasor, not RMS pressure. The original magma purple-to-yellow color map is restored. White curves show exact intersections of the existing vessel mesh with each plane. XY preserves equal spatial aspect. YZ and XZ are enlarged vertically for readability, with explicit mm coordinates; their unequal horizontal/vertical display scales are recorded in PROVENANCE.json. The two right panels have identical widths and heights, with a 13 mm gap. Their combined upper/lower edges align with the XY panel. This display aspect does not change the source coordinates, pressure samples or vessel geometry. Display ranges are X=[-3.2,10.2], Y=[-6.2,6.2] and Z=[-1.6,1.6] mm; X and Y limits match the preceding right-hand plane. The homogeneous-water acoustic model does not resolve vessel-wall material interfaces. No simulation was run and no original source data were modified. The figure contains no volume rendering, isosurfaces or volume ROI locator. PDF/SVG use raster field maps and editable vector labels and vessel outlines.
