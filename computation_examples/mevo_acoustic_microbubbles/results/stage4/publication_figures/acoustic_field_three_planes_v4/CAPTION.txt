Orthogonal sections of the B2 acoustic pressure amplitude at 2.5 MHz. The XY, YZ and XZ planes are extracted at Z=0, X=0 and Y=0 mm, respectively, from the existing complex acoustic field. Every section uses native 0.1 mm grid nodes without field smoothing or interpolation; the raster display uses nearest-neighbor sampling. The shared linear color scale is 0-39 kPa and represents |P|, the magnitude of the complex pressure phasor, not RMS pressure. The original magma purple-to-yellow color map is restored. White curves show exact intersections of the existing vessel mesh with each plane. Every panel preserves equal spatial aspect, so the vessel sections are not stretched. YZ and XZ use taller coordinate windows with Z=[-2.3,2.3] mm. The original field only covers Z=[-1.6,1.6] mm; the additional light-grey bands marked Outside stored field contain no data, not zero-valued pressure, and no field extrapolation is performed. The right panels have identical widths and an approximately 12.92 mm gap. Their combined upper/lower edges align with the XY panel. The source coordinates, pressure samples and vessel geometry are unchanged. X=[-3.2,10.2] and Y=[-6.2,6.2] mm match the preceding figure; the Z coordinate window is expanded only for layout. The homogeneous-water acoustic model does not resolve vessel-wall material interfaces. No simulation was run and no original source data were modified. The figure contains no volume rendering, isosurfaces or volume ROI locator. PDF/SVG use raster field maps and editable vector labels and vessel outlines.
