COM, Rg, pair distances and structure-factor errors are stored. Additional 58 shared-time comparisons give maximum cumulative-angle difference 4.70657e-08 deg and first-contact timing difference 2.19615 us; these event times are not claimed identical. Gain ranks are evaluated within each independent state, avoiding cross-state baseline-score inflation. Contact fallback can remove most of the speedup. No HF-positive pattern exists, so observed zero false negatives does NOT establish sensitivity. Formal pattern-based pruning remains unadmitted.
