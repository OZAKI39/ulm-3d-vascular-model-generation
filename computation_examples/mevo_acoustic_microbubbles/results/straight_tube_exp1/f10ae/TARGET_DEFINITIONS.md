# Target definitions

T1: aspect gain 3% ±0.5 percentage points; COM ≤20 nm; complete numerical trajectory. The three labels remain independent. T2: aspect gain 0.4% ±0.2 percentage points and endpoint connected; all-time connectivity separate. T3: an absolute sorted principal RMS length increase above max(1 nm, 5 times dt/cache noise); distribution splitting is separated from connected-group deformation. Near-degenerate orientations are invalidated.

T4: >=50% recovery and final relative Rg / all-axis errors ≤0.5%, aligned covariance error ≤1%, fixed-ID Kabsch RMS ≤5 nm, neighbor Jaccard ≥0.95. Each recovery level is separate and must improve on OFF. These criteria were frozen in protocol.json before trajectories. Fixed IDs are never reassigned.
