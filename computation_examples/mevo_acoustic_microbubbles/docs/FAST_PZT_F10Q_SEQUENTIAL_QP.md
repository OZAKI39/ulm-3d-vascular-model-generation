# F10Q Sequential QP

This is an opt-in numerical discretization of the same exploratory frictionless, nonadhesive spherical-particle model, not new contact physics. Historical F10P, B2 and M0 files remain byte-identical.

For one physical step, freeze external acoustic/secondary forces F(x_n) and the original F10G symmetric positive-definite pair resistance A(x_n). In dimensionless displacement s=(x_new-x_n)/R, solve the original objective 0.5 sᵀ A s − bᵀ s with b=dt F/(ζR). At each candidate geometry q, the affine constraint is B(q)s + [g(q)+B(q)(x_n−q)]/R ≥ 0. Each solve proposes x_new=x_n+Rs from the SAME x_n; it never advances another dt.

Recompute exact pair distances and actual normals at x_new. The physical pair multiplier is λ=ζR λ_scaled/dt. Check local QP KKT conditions and independently check ζ A v − F − B(x_new)ᵀλ, mapped through A⁻¹ as a mobility residual as well as in newtons. Check both local and actual reaction-weighted complementarity max|λ g| / max(max_i|F_i|, max|λ|, 1e−18 N). This is not simply the maximum geometric gap. Negative reactions, local inequality violation, original correction budget and original GPU cavity checks also reject a step.

Named adapter gates are LOCAL_QP_PASS, TRUE_GEOMETRY_PASS, TRUE_GAP_COMPLEMENTARITY_PASS, CONTACT_REACTION_BALANCE_PASS, CONTACT_CORRECTION, WALL_PASS and FULL_STEP_ACCEPTED. Pressure response stays strict at 2.5 MHz; zero pressure is explicitly supported, nonzero pressure must remain in the original APECSS range. The field interpolation and F2 force evaluation are unchanged. No PBD, soft spring, force clipping, added flow, or wall projection is applied to rescue a step.

The first frozen replay used at most three QPs. Its true-normal force residual decreased from 7.204e−12 to 1.646e−13 to 4.107e−15 N, but still failed the original force gate. The archived failed replay justified algorithm_revision_1: a cap of five QPs, with identical tolerances. The actual frozen failure passes on QP four. All original F10P small references plus independent nonlinear KKT root references for tangential motion, compression/sliding, release, four simultaneous contacts and active-set change pass. Independent references neither call the production QP nor the production contact geometry. They check a local constrained solution, not universal global uniqueness.

Full trajectories are fresh integrations from the original 32-ID initial condition. A 150-step pilot and 0.5 ms strong run pass. The strong 2 ms attempt stops at the 0.884 ms proposal with an active-set cycle in its first local QP; only 0.883 ms is accepted. The failing QP output is retained but is never a physical state. No strong half-step/full fine-cache trajectory was run because the registered full coarse trajectory failed. Route B remains independent.

The limits remain 17.8 nm original residual overlap, 1 nm hard diagnostic, 178 nm contact correction, 1e−13 m mobility residual, 1e−12 m local inequality and weighted complementarity, 1e−11 m actual weighted complementarity, and the original 1e−16 N + 1e−8×force_scale force balance. Numerical sphere geometry remains subject to floating-point roundoff. Temporal convergence and physical calibration are separate requirements.

References: [OSQP algorithm documentation](https://osqp.org/docs/solver/index.html), [SciPy constrained optimization](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.html). F10Q uses the already validated F10P active-set QP, the original F10G resistance, and explicitly recorded bounded relinearization; general convex-QP uniqueness does not imply unique or experimentally correct many-body evolution.
