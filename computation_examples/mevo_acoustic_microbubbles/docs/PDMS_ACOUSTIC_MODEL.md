# F10M spatial medium and evidence limits

The material reference is `SYLGARD184_REFERENCE_ASSUMPTION`, not confirmation of the user's brand, cure or acoustic properties. Source: Xu et al., Physical Review Applied 13, 054069 (2020), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.13.054069), [author-hosted full text](https://www.bruus-lab.dk/TMF/publications/Bruus/Bruus_153.pdf). Tables II/III and equations 17/18 were read and Table III visually inspected. We select the single 10:1, 85°C group: density 1031.4 ± 1.0 kg/m³, longitudinal phase speed at 5 MHz 1029 m/s, amplitude attenuation at 5 MHz 214.8 Np/m, power exponent 1.494. Measurement temperature was 25°C; samples aged 10 days. Cure duration is not supplied here and remains null. The article describes a volume mixing ratio; the user's actual ratio convention must be confirmed. [Dow's product page](https://www.dow.com/zh-cn/pdp.sylgard-184-silicone-elastomer-kit.01064291z.html) is a product reference, not an ultrasound calibration table.

Measured frequency coverage is 3–7 MHz. No values are silently extrapolated to 2.5 MHz or all of 1–7 MHz. The planned whole-apparatus field uses 3 MHz as the lowest measured reference frequency, not a confirmed experimental setting. A bounded adapter test uses 5 MHz; neither has a compatible old bubble response table. The OFF-only bubble test explicitly remains `FREQUENCY_DEMO_2P5MHZ` and does not use a PDMS field.

The 1.5 mm open top port is unioned with the main liquid channel; maps explicitly remove this volume from PDMS. Three material IDs are stored on the physical acoustic grid: 0 exterior, 1 PDMS, 2 channel liquid. Exterior and channel liquid are explicitly lossless water demonstrations (1500 m/s, 1000 kg/m³, zero attenuation); their actual composition and temperature remain unknown. Material assignment uses cell-center/node coordinates, so curved interfaces are staircased at finite grid spacing; exact geometry remains separate. No claim of interface/grid convergence is made.

The installed k-wave-python 0.6.2 Python/CuPy backend was inspected. It expands speed, density and absorption coefficient maps, but reads only the first value of `alpha_power`. The new adapter rejects non-scalar exponents and incompatible regional exponents. It also rejects bad shapes, nonpositive density/speed, negative losses, checksum mismatches and the dispersive y=1 singularity. C++ heterogeneous source scaling is explicitly not admitted by this adapter.

`alpha_coeff` is dB/(MHz^y cm), whereas the paper gives amplitude Np/m. Convert with `alpha_ref * (20 / ln(10)) / 100 / f_ref_MHz**y`. The shared y=1.494 applies to nonzero PDMS loss. Zero-loss liquids have no conflicting fitted exponent. Absorption and dispersion are both enabled. The input zero-frequency speed c0 is derived from equation 18: `1/c0 = 1/c_ref - alpha_ref*tan(pi*y/2)/(2*pi*f_ref)`. Feeding the frequency-dependent phase speed directly while also enabling dispersion would double-count it. The predicted 5 MHz phase was checked against actual GPU output. This weak-loss constitutive conversion is a reference approximation, not new material measurements.

`medium.sound_speed_map`, `medium.density_map` and `medium.alpha_coeff_map` map to the installed API's array-valued `sound_speed`, `density`, `alpha_coeff`; HDF5 maps additionally require SHA-256. `KWaveCW` chooses dt from maximum speed and settling time from minimum speed. Positive-time phasors are `Re[P exp(+iωt)]`. The existing F6 derivative of the same trilinear complex P is retained and checked. Source pressure amplitudes are numerical inputs, not local pressure, voltage or electrical power.

PZT sources remain transparent additive faces. They do not model thickness, backing, reflection, electrode response, bonding layers or port sealing. The PDMS model is a longitudinal equivalent fluid, not full solid elasticity: shear waves, mode conversion and a full elastic boundary problem are absent. No complete elastic propagation, hydrophone validation, source transfer calibration, streaming or thermal effects are claimed.

The complete 3 MHz apparatus estimate exceeds the 4090 memory/time budgets before any solve. The top injection port and closed X+ end are now user-confirmed. Its 5.4 mm measurement reference, lateral centering and blind depth remain provisional, and actual solid-end acoustics remain uncalibrated. Whole-apparatus sources and ON trajectories are therefore `NOT_RUN`. Real planar and miniature-disc adapter validations are separately labeled and must never be presented as the 32.5 mm apparatus field. No 1–7 MHz frequency sweep, no previous fields recomputed, and no AI training.
