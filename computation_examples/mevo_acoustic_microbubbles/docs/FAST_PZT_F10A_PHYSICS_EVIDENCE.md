# F10A physics evidence and scope

## Pressure and parameter identity

Source commands multiply four stored k-Wave basis fields. Their units are source-pressure commands, not measured hardware voltages. Local pressure is the magnitude of the complex peak phasor after superposition and trilinear sampling. For the prescribed single sinusoid the negative peak magnitude equals that amplitude and RMS equals amplitude/sqrt(2); this relation need not hold for an experimental distorted waveform. Sum of squared source commands is a comparison proxy, not acoustic watts or electrical power. The 20,000 Pa/channel exploratory cap was registered before the runs; historical 291.2376877 Pa defaults remain unchanged.

The production table actually uses Tu Figure 4 manually digitized size interpolation: R0=1.78 µm, chi=0.2932519822 N/m, kappa_s=4.619149127e-9 kg/s. This differs from the task's Table 2 fit (chi=0.30, kappa_s=3.2e-9), which is solved separately. Both main references assume sigma_init=0.036 N/m. No production table is relabelled or regenerated. APECSS uses effective radial liquid viscosity 0.002 Pa s; translation uses 0.001 Pa s, rho=1000 kg/m³, c=1500 m/s, p0=101300 Pa, gas exponent1.07. Gas dissolution and irreversible lipid loss are absent.

## Numerical criteria and limits

Direct APECSS uses the pinned v1.8 library and unmodified official ultrasound driver,48 cycles with512 samples/cycle maximum step;3 and10 kPa production checks are repeated at1024. The last8 cycles supply the stored waveform and8 volume harmonics; upstream -p*sin(wt) is converted to the positive-exponential cosine phasor by dividing harmonic n by i**n. Exact zero response and out-of-table pressure rejection are tested. Direct production/table complex fundamental and derivative-energy errors must each be <=5%, matching the existing table's registered interpolation tolerance; this is not experimental validation. Auxiliary sigma=0/.072 cases use direct solutions only.

M0 includes Stokes plus existing local lubrication and component mean balance; a diagonal predictor therefore gives the same group mean, but not individual velocities. The latter are reconstructed and checked against the actual production GPU path. Free-space RPY is reported only as static sensitivity, without near-wall truth claims. Static frozen-velocity wall forecasts are support probes, not simulated paths. The stored-field box residence estimate is an upper bound, not a guaranteed CFD streamline lifetime.

Two candidate budgets are checked simultaneously: exact initial median pressure and source-command squared sum no greater than S; O maximum local pressure may exceed S by at most1%. Every accepted candidate rechecks table and F2 domain errors. The search is finite and local; failure to find positive drift is not a proof of global impossibility. Iteration-limited results are retained as feasible candidates, not certified optima.
