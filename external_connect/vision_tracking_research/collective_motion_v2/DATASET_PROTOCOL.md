# F10V dataset protocol

One source recording: `Recording 2026-10-15 185901 截取视频.mp4`, SHA-256 `3fc13aaa611a4eeb94b27804524318b3a8411cc38967804c6620913cfb9c484c`. There is no independent recording/session holdout: **SINGLE_RECORDING_GENERALIZATION_NOT_VERIFIED**.

`configs/protocol.json` preregisters four disjoint material frame pools, four disjoint spatial ROIs, and disjoint random-seed ranges. Training uses 8,192 triples; validation 1,024; calibration 1,024; final test 1,536. Each sample has a unique deterministic seed. Synthetic replicates are not independent physical experiments. Source regions are disjoint, but source recording and imaging apparatus are shared. F10U historical test used frame 840 and ROI [650,300,256,256]; the new final-test material uses frames 780/800/820/860 and ROI [930,680,256,256]. Old tests/results remain frozen, and shared recording prevents a new cross-experiment claim.

F10U `case_params`, homogeneous `generator`, real frame decoding, frozen `CandidateDetector`, and feathered real-patch construction are reused. New bounded splatting reproduces the old full-canvas warp in a unit test. We extend the generator for independently timed causal triples, start/stop, acceleration, known nonlinear motion, and four material splits. Real 21×21 local image patches are feathered; threshold contours are not treated as exact bubble masks. Patch contours, stationary source dots and limited depth/focus variation remain synthetic-domain artifacts. The preview and final Figure 2 expose these limitations.

Each triple is one continuous process. Times are [0, dt01, dt01+dt12], with each interval selected from 0.01665, 0.0333, 0.05 seconds. Constant generators use expm; start/stop/acceleration use ordered piecewise transforms. All affine labels are the interval-equivalent logm(H02)/(t2−t0), not instantaneous velocity. Fixed ROI center is (128,128), x right/y down, positive vorticity clockwise. Raw images are 256²; network inputs are 128². Non-square real ROIs use full affine coordinate conjugation, including the pixel-center offset.

All training heatmaps use actual detections on the rendered frames. Gaussian sigma is 3 raw pixels; isolated peaks are one, overlaps clip to one. Detector contrast is not calibrated probability. H4 and corrupted inference conditions alter the **detected** centers, never replace them with generator truth centers. H4 randomly drops/adds/jitters centers, broadens peaks and occasionally removes the heatmap channel.

Global affine difficulty cases retain global labels. A smooth nonlinear displacement is NON_AFFINE_MOTION only if grid affine-fit RMS exceeds 1.5 px. Explicit absent endpoint content, <1-gray-level contrast or degenerate collinear support define NOT_OBSERVABLE. Sparse/blurred/background-dominant images are not automatically labeled unobservable. This label policy is a controlled synthetic operational definition, not perfect real-image observability ground truth.

Final-test files were generated/hashed before training and not used for checkpoint choice, threshold selection or calibration. Selection is validation-only; the freeze record precedes final predictions. Independent calibration uses source-frame × motion-case groups; these groups share a recording and are not independent experiments. All scientific comparison limits remain [5,5,0.2,0.2,0.1,0.1].
