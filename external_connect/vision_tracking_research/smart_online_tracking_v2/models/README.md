# External base models

Base checkpoints and embeddings were excluded from this code/data snapshot. Exact original paths, sizes and SHA-256 are in EXTERNAL_MODEL_MANIFEST.json. Follow ../MODEL_AND_ENVIRONMENT.md and the existing preannotation setup to obtain the matching upstream checkpoints. Promoted project-specific SAM decoders and selected trained Cellpose candidates are included under ../results and ../microscope_sessions, together with their validation/promotion decisions. Do not automatically promote rejected candidates.
